## Interpretation

A one-cell result is not automatically an error. It means the regional profile plus the clean parent area currently rounds/clamps to one cell. The important review groups are (a) fine/mixed-level source features that may need an explicit level policy and (b) unusually large ordinary Admin-1 that still receive one cell.
